## Prompt

```text
Gameplay:
- Arrow keys or WASD to drive. On phones, show on-screen left, right and gas buttons.
- Collect glowing cash bags around the city. Each one raises a heat meter from 1 to 5 stars.
- More heat means more police cars chasing you with sirens. They try to box you in.
- Touching a police car ends the run. Collect as much as you can.
- Show cash, heat level and best score. After a bust, show "Busted! Press Space to try again".

Look and feel:
- Night city with palm trees, wet streets reflecting pink and cyan neon, and a beach road along the ocean.
- Headlights, skid marks when drifting, flashing red and blue police lights.
- Smooth animation that fills the browser window.

Use only original names and art. No real brands, logos or characters.

Before you finish, test the controls, the chase and restarting on desktop and phone screen sizes. Then show me the game.
```
